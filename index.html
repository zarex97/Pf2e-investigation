About this codex

Every base class in Pathfinder Second Edition as of Impossible Magic (July 2026) — 29 in all. Each entry covers the mechanical engine you actually operate turn to turn, and the character the class asks you to play.

Action symbols follow the game’s own notation: ◆ one action, ◆◆ two actions, ◆◆◆ three actions, ↻ reaction, ◇ free action.

The ten bars come from Classfinder 2e and run 0–5. Each axis keeps its own colour and glyph everywhere it appears, so you can read a class’s shape without checking the labels:

Eight of the ten are capabilities, where a longer bar is better. The last two are not, and they are deliberately drawn in muted greys so a tall bar never reads as “stronger”: Difficulty is a cost — how much the class asks of its player — and Popularity is simply how often it gets played.
